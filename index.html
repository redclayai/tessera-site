Windows, remembered.

Tessera saves your window layouts and brings them back in one click. Snap windows into zones you draw, apply any layout from the keyboard, and let Claude tidy your desktop. A native app for macOS.

What it does

Layouts

Save any arrangement of your windows across displays and restore it in one click, from the menu bar, or with a shortcut. Undo the last apply.

Snapping

Drag to edges for halves and quarters, or draw your own zones per display and hold ⇧ while dragging. Gaps, and resize neighbours together.

Quick-apply palette

Press ⌃⌥P, type a layout name, press Return.

Placement rules

"Always open Mail in zone 3." New windows land where you want them.

Ask Claude

Optional local server so your own Claude client can arrange windows. It can move and resize, never close or quit.

Sync & automation

iCloud sync for layouts and zones, triggers by display, time or app launch, Shortcuts actions and a URL scheme.

Private by design

Everything stays on your Mac. No account, no analytics, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.

Requirements

macOS 26 or later. Tessera asks for Accessibility access to move other apps' windows and Screen Recording access to read their window titles. Help & support.
