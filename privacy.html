Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026 · Red Clay AI, Inc. ("we") · App: Tessera: Window Layouts for macOS

The short version

Tessera does not collect, transmit, sell or share your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything the app stores stays on your Mac.

What the app stores, and where

To do its job, Tessera reads the names, titles, positions and sizes of your open windows and saves them as layouts on your Mac, together with your zones, placement rules and settings. This information is stored locally in the app's own storage and is never sent to us or to any third party.

Permissions

  • Accessibility: to read and set the position and size of other apps' windows.
  • Screen Recording: only to read other apps' window titles so windows can be matched when a layout is restored. Tessera does not capture, record or store screen contents.

Optional features

  • iCloud sync (off by default): if you turn it on, your layouts, zones and rules are stored in your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud service. We cannot access them. Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud.
  • Claude connection (off by default): if you turn it on, Tessera runs a server that accepts connections only from your own Mac and requires an access token. The MCP client you choose (for example Claude) can then list and move windows. Tessera does not send anything to us. What your client does with that information is governed by that client's own privacy policy.
  • Layout actions: a layout can open URLs or files you specify when it is applied.

Children

Tessera does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
