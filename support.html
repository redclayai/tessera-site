Getting started

Tessera can't move my windows

Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility and turn Tessera on. Also turn it on under Screen Recording (used only to read window titles). Quit and reopen Tessera afterwards.

How do I save and restore a layout?

Arrange your windows, click + in the Layouts toolbar, and name it. To restore, click Apply Layout on its card, use the menu bar, or press ⌃⌥P and type its name. The toolbar's Undo button puts windows back.

How do I snap into my own zones?

In Snapping, turn on "Drag windows to screen edges", draw zones in the Custom Zones editor, then hold ⇧ while dragging a window by its title bar. Hold ⌘ as well to span several zones.

Common questions

A shortcut doesn't work or conflicts with another app

Tessera's snapping shortcuts are off until you turn them on, and every shortcut can be changed in Snapping › Keyboard Shortcuts. If you use Magnet, Rectangle or macOS tiling, turn those features off so only one app responds.

Some apps won't go to the exact size

A few apps enforce a minimum or maximum window size. Tessera places them as close as the app allows.

Sync with iCloud

Turn it on in Settings › General on each Mac, signed in to the same Apple ID with iCloud Drive enabled.

Connecting Claude

In Settings › Claude, turn on "Let Claude arrange my windows" and copy the setup for Claude Code or Claude Desktop. The server only accepts connections from your Mac and requires the access token shown there.
